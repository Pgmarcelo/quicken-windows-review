# Account classification and access checks

Use for every review. Cover all available accounts in a full review; in a narrow follow-up, check affected accounts and say what was not reassessed. Refresh evidence rather than copying prior classifications. This workflow produces recommendations, not permission to change Quicken records.

## Identify what each account represents

Keep six things separate: Quicken's underlying register type; Account Intent/display group; the real product and purpose; its legal tax wrapper; the underlying investment mix; and access to money for bills. A banking QIF type may not distinguish checking from savings. A Retirement heading does not establish tax treatment, and a fund label does not establish stock/bond exposure or risk.

Record a masked account identifier, ownership share, provider/product, Quicken type and intent when observable, native currency, native and report-currency values, statement/value date, exchange-rate basis, report inclusion, and hidden/separate/closed status where available. Mark missing fields unknown. An account omitted from a report is not proven zero or closed.

Use exports and statements first. When settings affect the recommendation, inspect Account List and Account Details read-only through the available computer-use skill; record source and observation date. Discover actual menus each session. Do not assume every option in general documentation exists for this account or edition. If access is blocked, identify the unverified fields and continue supported analysis.

## Recommend the classification on evidence

Provide an account-by-account table: current setup; proposed economic purpose and supported display group; balance/currency/date; evidence and confidence; missing information; and a specific next action with a completion check. Include correctly classified accounts as “keep as is.” Label name-based hypotheses and verify the exact product on statements before relying on them.

- Separate operating bank cash from investments and pensions even when all are grouped under Banking or Savings.
- For a fixed-income instrument, verify issuer, maturity, withdrawal terms, fees, tax withholding and net amount available. Daily access in the native country does not guarantee same-day money in the currency needed for bills.
- Verify a fund's identifier, holdings/risk, fees and withdrawal delay. A name implying fixed income does not establish capital protection.
- Keep retirement-purpose assets outside the immediate reserve until access is established. Never map a foreign pension to a domestic tax-advantaged plan just to get a Retirement heading, or toggle Tax deferred to force presentation. Confirm jurisdiction-specific treatment before tax guidance.
- Keep credit cards and money the user owes as liabilities. Money owed to the user is a receivable/asset, subject to collectability. Confirm direction and settlement instead of relying on a name such as “loan.”
- Check whether property is still owned, sold, or represented by a remaining receivable. Match sale documents and settlement transfers; similar amounts alone do not prove duplicates.
- Review near-zero legacy balances against final statements. Do not delete history, post dummy income to force zero, or recommend closure solely because a balance is small.

Use current provider/regulator sources for unfamiliar product definitions while distinguishing general definitions from the user's exact contract. Do not infer tax residency from currency, geography, provider or language. Keep ledgers in native currency and convert once for the report. Do not add different currencies, treat native amounts as report amounts, or reconvert already translated values. A current exchange rate does not make a stale investment value current.

## Check transaction labels as well as headings

Inspect balance/valuation adjustments, generic income categories, credited interest/distributions, contributions, redemptions, sale proceeds and transfers. Retain date, native amount/currency, category and source location for flagged entries in private analysis outputs.

An increase in investment value is not evidence of cash received. Distinguish unrealized growth, actually credited income, transfers, withdrawals containing original capital, and corrections using statements. Do not rename all entries with a shared description automatically. Show uncertain items separately in the cash budget; do not silently rewrite reported income, remove genuine distributions, or subtract native-currency adjustments from reporting-currency totals. Explain effects on budget, performance and tax reports separately from net worth.

## Explain safe options and their limits

Offer the least disruptive supported option first:

1. **Display/name/report-selection correction:** inspect Tools → Account List → Edit (or the observed equivalent) → Display Options → Account Intent. Recommend only available options. A suitable Asset intent may improve presentation for a bank-style investment record, but does not convert it into an investment register or create holdings, purchase costs or performance tracking. Check cash-flow/report-selection effects.
2. **Detailed investment tracking:** if the register cannot represent the product, propose a separately planned manual investment account in native currency with a supported type and verified holding/history data. Do not promise in-place conversion or fabricate acquisition costs.

Before any separately authorized implementation, require a dated backup, baseline reports, verified ownership/currency/product and a clear cutover/history plan. Preserve historical transfers and values. Do not count both legacy balances and replacement holdings, zero/close a register prematurely, or turn migration into fictional purchases/sales. Resolve material uncertainty before applying a change. The review itself remains read-only.

Keep owned investments in consolidated net worth. Do not select “Keep this account separate” merely to tidy the display if it would remove owned assets from reports. Use appropriate account/report selection for the immediate-cash budget; distinguish display hiding from report exclusion.

Compare before/after reports at the same date, scope, ownership and exchange rates. A pure grouping/name change should preserve net worth. Explain differences from corrected scope, ownership, valuation or accounting separately. Verify account grouping, cash-budget inclusion and relevant income/tax reports, not just the sidebar. Separate proposed changes from completed ones.

## Explain the financial effect

Where supported, show reported bank/cash, investment-purpose amounts currently grouped there, and an illustrative operating-cash subtotal. Label this a proposed reclassification, not new money, an executed change, or a verified emergency reserve. Reconcile back to the original total, explain residuals and avoid adding products already included in a portfolio report. Withhold consolidated subtotals if ownership, conversion or product identity remains unresolved.

For reserve coverage, subtract upcoming commitments from eligible accessible cash and divide by supported essential monthly needs. Keep withdrawal limits, foreign-transfer timing, taxes and fees explicit. If access or essentials are unknown, leave months covered unavailable.

Reusable skill and public repository files contain only general instructions or explicitly synthetic examples. Keep actual account inventories/names, figures, transactions, statements, screenshots, reports and personal source locations in private local outputs. Never copy them into skill files or GitHub commit messages.
